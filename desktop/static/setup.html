Lunar Landing Site Navigator

Terrain maps

The navigator computes the Sun and the Earth for any place and date on the Moon already. NASA's terrain maps add the real skyline (mountains and crater rims that hide the Sun or the Earth), the slope and the surface data. Choose once; you can change it later from the Maps menu.

Use maps I already have

Pick the folder with the maps: a maps folder (it contains dem), or a project folder that has data/raw/maps. Nothing is downloaded.

Continue without maps

Everything works, anywhere, but with the horizon of a smooth Moon: no mountains or craters on the skyline, and no slope, temperature or rock data. Near the poles the results are optimistic.

Base 3 GB

Real terrain skyline everywhere, slope and altitude, and NASA's long-term Sun and Earth visibility and shadow maps near the south pole.

Complete 13.7 GB

Everything in Base, plus the 5 m terrain around the south pole, surface temperatures, rock abundance and thermal inertia (H-parameter). The full analysis.
